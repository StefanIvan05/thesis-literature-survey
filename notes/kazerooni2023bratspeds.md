# Fathi Kazerooni et al., 2023, arXiv

- **BibTeX key:** `kazerooni2023bratspeds`
- **Category:** Dataset (pediatric brain MRI)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17033
- **PDF:** [`papers/kazerooni2023bratspeds.pdf`](../papers/kazerooni2023bratspeds.pdf)

## Summary
Describes BraTS-PEDs 2023, the first BraTS challenge on pediatric brain tumours (high-grade gliomas), with multi-institutional multi-parametric MRI and expert sub-region segmentations.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Pediatric brain tumours are a less-explored niche than adult glioma, which could allow a more original contribution, for example transfer from adult BraTS to pediatric data.

## Where I will use it
Dataset chapter; possible research question on adult-to-pediatric transfer.

## Limitations / open questions
Smaller than adult BraTS; 3D segmentation is computationally demanding.
