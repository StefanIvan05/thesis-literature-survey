# Zhao et al., 2022, arXiv

- **BibTeX key:** `zhao2022mmotu`
- **Category:** Dataset (ovarian ultrasound)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06799
- **PDF:** [`papers/zhao2022mmotu.pdf`](../papers/zhao2022mmotu.pdf)

## Summary
Introduces MMOTU: 1,469 2D ultrasound images and 170 contrast-enhanced ultrasound (CEUS) images of ovarian tumours with pixel-level and image-level annotations, used for unsupervised cross-domain segmentation.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
A public dataset for the ovarian-ultrasound direction. Because it is 2D, it is much cheaper to train on than 3D MRI, and the two modalities allow domain-shift experiments.

## Where I will use it
Dataset chapter for the ovarian direction; possible domain-generalisation research question.

## Limitations / open questions
Single-centre and relatively small. I found a Zenodo copy (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21128657) uploaded by other authors, so I must check which version is official and how it is licensed.
