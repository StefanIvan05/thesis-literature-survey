# Liang et al., 2025, Scientific Data

- **BibTeX key:** `liang2025utendomri`
- **Category:** Dataset (endometriosis MRI)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05623-3
- **PDF:** [`papers/liang2025utendomri.pdf`](../papers/liang2025utendomri.pdf)

## Summary
Describes UT-EndoMRI, a multi-center pelvic MRI dataset (multiple MRI sequences) with manual segmentations of pelvic organs, including the uterus, ovaries and endometriomas, for deep-learning segmentation in endometriosis.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
The main public dataset for the endometriosis direction, released under CC BY 4.0. Endometriosis imaging is an under-explored area, so there is room for a contribution.

## Where I will use it
Dataset chapter for the gynaecology direction.

## Limitations / open questions
Recent and relatively small; segmentation labels may vary between annotators.
