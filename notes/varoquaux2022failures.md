# Varoquaux & Cheplygina, 2022, npj Digital Medicine

- **BibTeX key:** `varoquaux2022failures`
- **Category:** Overview / methodology
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-022-00592-y
- **PDF:** [`papers/varoquaux2022failures.pdf`](../papers/varoquaux2022failures.pdf)

## Summary
Reviews systematic problems in ML for medical imaging: dataset bias, too-small test sets, overfitting to public benchmarks, data leakage, incorrect evaluation and research incentives that favour small 'state-of-the-art' gains. Gives recommendations for more reliable research.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Acts as a checklist of mistakes to avoid in my own experiments, for example splitting data by patient rather than by image, reporting confidence intervals, and not over-tuning on the test set.

## Where I will use it
Methodology chapter (experimental design, evaluation protocol); Threats to Validity section.

## Limitations / open questions
A position/review paper: it gives principles, not a ready-made protocol for a specific dataset.
