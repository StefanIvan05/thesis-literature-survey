# Huang et al., 2020, npj Digital Medicine

- **BibTeX key:** `huang2020fusion`
- **Category:** Multimodal AI
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-020-00341-z
- **PDF:** [`papers/huang2020fusion.pdf`](../papers/huang2020fusion.pdf)

## Summary
Systematic review of studies that fuse medical images with electronic health record (EHR) data. Classifies fusion strategies as early, joint (intermediate) and late fusion, and finds that multimodal models usually outperform single-modality ones. Gives implementation guidelines.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
The key reference for the multimodal idea suggested by the industry expert (images plus lab results and patient history). It gives me the vocabulary and design options for fusion.

## Where I will use it
Related Work (multimodal); Methods if I combine images and clinical data.

## Limitations / open questions
Most included studies were small and retrospective; public datasets with paired images and EHR data are rare.
