# Tan & Le, 2019, ICML

- **BibTeX key:** `tan2019efficientnet`
- **Category:** Architectures (classification)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11946
- **PDF:** [`papers/tan2019efficientnet.pdf`](../papers/tan2019efficientnet.pdf)

## Summary
Proposes compound scaling of network depth, width and input resolution, and the EfficientNet family, which reaches high ImageNet accuracy with far fewer parameters than earlier CNNs.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
A second, more parameter-efficient baseline to compare with ResNet. This matters because I may have limited GPU resources.

## Where I will use it
Background (architectures); model comparison in experiments.

## Limitations / open questions
Efficiency was measured on ImageNet and does not automatically carry over to medical datasets.
