- **BibTeX key:** `christiansen2021ovarian`

## Summary
Deep neural networks (ensembles of ImageNet pre-trained models) classified ovarian tumours as benign or malignant from ultrasound images, with diagnostic performance comparable to expert subjective assessment.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Directly relevant to the ovarian-ultrasound direction. It shows that the task is feasible and gives a clinical baseline (expert examiners) to compare against. It also supports the industry feedback that ultrasound is hard to read.

## Where I will use it
Related Work for the gynaecology direction; choosing evaluation metrics (sensitivity/specificity).

## Limitations / open questions
Their dataset is private, so the study is not reproducible with public data. Open access (CC BY), but the PDF download was blocked, so link only.
