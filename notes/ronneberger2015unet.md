# Ronneberger et al., 2015, MICCAI

- **BibTeX key:** `ronneberger2015unet`
- **Category:** Architectures (segmentation)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-24574-4_28
- **PDF:** [`papers/ronneberger2015unet.pdf`](../papers/ronneberger2015unet.pdf)

## Summary
U-Net: an encoder-decoder network with skip connections for biomedical image segmentation, trainable end-to-end from very few annotated images with heavy data augmentation.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Standard architecture if my topic involves segmentation, i.e. delimiting a tumour or endometrioma rather than only classifying the image.

## Where I will use it
Background (segmentation); baseline for any segmentation experiment.

## Limitations / open questions
Original evaluation was on microscopy (2D); 3D MRI needs 3D variants.
