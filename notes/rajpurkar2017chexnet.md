# Rajpurkar et al., 2017, arXiv

- **BibTeX key:** `rajpurkar2017chexnet`
- **Category:** Applications
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05225
- **PDF:** [`papers/rajpurkar2017chexnet.pdf`](../papers/rajpurkar2017chexnet.pdf)

## Summary
CheXNet, a 121-layer DenseNet trained on the ChestX-ray14 dataset (over 100,000 X-rays), reported pneumonia detection exceeding the average of four radiologists on F1 score.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
An example of a high-impact claim that was later criticised, for example over label quality and generalisation (see Zech 2018). Useful for critically discussing how results should be evaluated and reported.

## Where I will use it
Related Work; Discussion (critical reading of headline results).

## Limitations / open questions
Preprint; noisy labels obtained by text-mining reports; small radiologist comparison.
