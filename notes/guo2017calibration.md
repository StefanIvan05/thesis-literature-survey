# Guo et al., 2017, ICML

- **BibTeX key:** `guo2017calibration`
- **Category:** Reliability (uncertainty)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599
- **PDF:** [`papers/guo2017calibration.pdf`](../papers/guo2017calibration.pdf)

## Summary
Shows that modern deep networks are over-confident (poorly calibrated) and that temperature scaling, a single post-hoc parameter, fixes most of the miscalibration.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
In medicine the confidence of a prediction matters, not only the label. Calibration (e.g. Expected Calibration Error) is a possible evaluation dimension and a cheap improvement for my models.

## Where I will use it
Methods/evaluation metrics; a possible contribution direction (reliability, not just accuracy).

## Limitations / open questions
Evaluated on natural-image and text datasets, not medical data.
