# Bakas et al., 2018, arXiv

- **BibTeX key:** `bakas2018brats`
- **Category:** Dataset / benchmark (brain MRI)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02629
- **PDF:** [`papers/bakas2018brats.pdf`](../papers/bakas2018brats.pdf)

## Summary
Summarises the BraTS challenges (2012-2018): multi-parametric MRI of gliomas with expert segmentations of tumour sub-regions, plus progression and survival-prediction tasks, and compares the submitted methods.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
BraTS is the reference benchmark for brain-tumour segmentation. It defines the standard MRI sequences, labels and metrics (Dice, Hausdorff distance) that I would use for the brain direction.

## Where I will use it
Related Work and Dataset chapters for the brain-tumour direction.

## Limitations / open questions
Adult gliomas only; 3D MRI requires significant compute.
