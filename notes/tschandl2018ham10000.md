# Tschandl et al., 2018, Scientific Data

- **BibTeX key:** `tschandl2018ham10000`
- **Category:** Dataset (dermatology)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2018.161
- **PDF:** [`papers/tschandl2018ham10000.pdf`](../papers/tschandl2018ham10000.pdf)

## Summary
Describes HAM10000: 10,015 dermatoscopic images of pigmented skin lesions in 7 diagnostic categories, collected from different populations and devices, with more than half confirmed by histopathology.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
A well-documented, freely available dataset for the skin-lesion option. The class imbalance (dominated by naevi) is a known challenge to address.

## Where I will use it
Dataset chapter, if dermatology is chosen.

## Limitations / open questions
Several images per lesion, so a split by lesion/patient is needed to avoid leakage; mostly lighter skin types.
