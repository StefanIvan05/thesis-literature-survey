# Pacheco et al., 2020, Data in Brief

- **BibTeX key:** `pacheco2020padufes`
- **Category:** Dataset (dermatology, multimodal)
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.106221
- **PDF:** not stored (see link)

## Summary
PAD-UFES-20: 2,298 smartphone images of skin lesions with patient clinical metadata (e.g. age, lesion location, symptoms, risk factors), covering six diagnostic classes.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
One of the few public datasets that pairs images with clinical data for the same patients, so it is a feasible way to test the multimodal idea.

## Where I will use it
Dataset chapter; multimodal experiments.

## Limitations / open questions
Smartphone images from one region of Brazil; relatively small. Open access, but the PDF download was blocked, so link only.
