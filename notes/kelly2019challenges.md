# Kelly et al., 2019, BMC Medicine

- **BibTeX key:** `kelly2019challenges`
- **Category:** Overview / clinical context
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-019-1426-2
- **PDF:** [`papers/kelly2019challenges.pdf`](../papers/kelly2019challenges.pdf)

## Summary
Discusses why few medical AI systems reach clinical practice: dataset shift, accidental fitting of confounders, algorithmic bias, lack of interpretability, metrics that do not reflect clinical usefulness, and regulatory/implementation barriers.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Provides the clinical motivation and the vocabulary for limitations. It supports my point that a model with high accuracy on one dataset is not a validated diagnostic tool.

## Where I will use it
Introduction (motivation) and Discussion/Limitations chapters.

## Limitations / open questions
Written from a clinical-translation viewpoint; it contains no experiments.
