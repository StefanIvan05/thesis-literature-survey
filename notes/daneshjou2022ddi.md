## Summary
Introduces the Diverse Dermatology Images (DDI) dataset with biopsy-confirmed lesions across skin tones. State-of-the-art dermatology models performed considerably worse on DDI than reported originally, especially on darker skin tones.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
Shows that dataset representativeness changes the conclusions, which is relevant if I choose dermatology (my supervisor's original suggestion) or study bias and generalisation in general.

## Where I will use it
Related Work (limitations of existing models); Discussion (bias).

## Limitations / open questions
Dermatology only; DDI is a test set and too small for training.
