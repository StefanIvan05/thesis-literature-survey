# Esteva et al., 2017, Nature

- **BibTeX key:** `esteva2017dermatologist`
- **Category:** Applications
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/nature21056
- **PDF:** not stored (see link)

## Summary
A CNN (Inception v3, ImageNet pre-trained) trained on about 129,000 clinical images matched board-certified dermatologists on two binary skin-cancer classification tasks.

## Why it is relevant to my thesis
A landmark paper and the origin of my supervisor's skin-cancer suggestion. It shows the standard transfer-learning recipe and the 'AI vs. specialists' evaluation style.

## Where I will use it
Introduction / Related Work (milestones).

## Limitations / open questions
Proprietary data, single-source evaluation; later work (Daneshjou 2022) showed generalisation problems. PDF not open access, so link only.
